### H3: Secant as the Reciprocal of Cosine

Begin with the cosine formula:

\[
\cos(A)=\frac{b}{c}
\]

Explain that taking the reciprocal reverses the numerator and denominator:

\[
\frac{1}{\cos(A)}=\frac{c}{b}
\]

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{\sec(A)=\frac{1}{\cos(A)}=\frac{c}{b}}
\]

Use this example:

\[
\cos(A)=\frac{4}{5}=0.8
\]

Then:

\[
\sec(A)=\frac{1}{0.8}=1.25
\]

Explain why cosine and secant describe the same angle using reciprocal ratios.

Clarify that secant is not inverse cosine. Inverse cosine is used to recover an angle from a cosine value.

### H3: What Does a Secant Value of 1.25 Mean?

Explain that:

\[
\sec(A)=1.25
\]

means the hypotenuse is 1.25 times as long as the adjacent side.

The percentage difference is:

\[
(1.25-1)\times100=25\%
\]

Therefore, the hypotenuse is 25% longer than the adjacent side.

Explain that this does not mean the angle is \(1.25^\circ\), nor does it mean the triangle has a length of 1.25 units.

### H3: Why Secant Is Greater Than 1

Explain that the hypotenuse is longer than either leg in a valid, non-degenerate right triangle.

Therefore:

\[
c>b>0
\]

Dividing \(c\) by \(b\) produces a ratio greater than 1:

\[
\boxed{\frac{c}{b}>1}
\]

Clarify that a result of exactly 1 is not possible for an ordinary acute reference angle in a non-degenerate right triangle.

The value approaches 1 as the angle approaches \(0^\circ\).

### H3: Why Secant Is the Reciprocal of Cosine

Explain the relationship using both formulas:

\[
\cos(A)=\frac{b}{c}
\]

\[
\sec(A)=\frac{c}{b}
\]

Show that multiplying them produces 1:

\[
\cos(A)\sec(A)=\frac{b}{c}\times\frac{c}{b}=1
\]

Explain that this reciprocal relationship is a useful way to verify a calculation.

### H3: Why Secant Has No Units

Explain that secant divides one length by another.

For example:

\[
\frac{5\text{ m}}{4\text{ m}}=1.25
\]

The metres cancel, leaving a dimensionless ratio.

Explain that the same ratio is obtained using 500 cm and 400 cm.

### H3: Secant Versus Cosecant

Explain that secant and cosecant are different reciprocal ratios.

Secant:

\[
\sec(A)=\frac{c}{b}
\]

Cosecant:

\[
\csc(A)=\frac{c}{a}
\]

Secant uses the adjacent side, while cosecant uses the opposite side.

## 13. USING SECANT TO FIND AN ANGLE

Explain that the secant ratio and the angle are different quantities.

\]

Then:

\[
A=\cos^{-1}(0.8)

Final answer:
