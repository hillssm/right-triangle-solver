### Essential mathematical distinctions

- Tangent equals opposite divided by adjacent.
- Sine equals opposite divided by hypotenuse.
- Cosine equals adjacent divided by hypotenuse.
- Cotangent is the reciprocal of tangent.
- The hypotenuse does not appear in the primary tangent formula.
- The opposite and adjacent labels depend on the selected reference angle.
- Tangent is a ratio, not an angle.
- Inverse tangent is a separate operation used to recover an angle from a known tangent ratio.
- Tangent is dimensionless because it divides one length by another.
- For a positive tangent ratio in a right triangle with an acute reference angle, the result is greater than zero.
- A tangent value of 1 means the opposite and adjacent sides are equal, so the acute reference angle is \(45^\circ\).
- A tangent value below 1 means the opposite side is shorter than the adjacent side.
- A tangent value above 1 means the opposite side is longer than the adjacent side.
- Tangent approaches zero as the angle approaches \(0^\circ\).
- Tangent grows without bound as the angle approaches \(90^\circ\).
- The tangent ratio equals rise divided by run when the opposite side represents vertical rise and the adjacent side represents horizontal run.
- Percentage grade is \(100\times\tan(A)\%\), when the angle is measured from the horizontal and the triangle models a straight incline.
