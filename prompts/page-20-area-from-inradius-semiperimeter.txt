### H3: Deriving the Right Triangle Inradius Formula

Explain the equal tangent-segment theorem.

From a common external point, the two tangent segments to a circle have equal lengths.

Let the tangent lengths from the three vertices be:

- x from vertex A.
- y from vertex B.
- z from vertex C.

For a right angle at C, the two tangent segments from C along the perpendicular legs each have length r.

Therefore:

\[
z=r
\]

The sides can be expressed as:

\[
a=y+r
\]

\[
b=x+r
\]

\[
c=x+y
\]

Subtract:

\[
a+b-c=(y+r)+(x+r)-(x+y)
\]

\[
a+b-c=2r
\]

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{r=\frac{a+b-c}{2}}
\]

Explain why this shortcut is specific to right triangles.
