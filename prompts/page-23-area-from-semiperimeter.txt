### H3: Why Does the Inradius Formula Work?

Use the tangent properties of a circle.

Explain:

1. Tangent segments drawn from the same external point have equal lengths.
2. The incircle touches both perpendicular legs.
3. The distances from the right-angle vertex to the two leg tangency points are each r.
4. The remaining tangent segments along the legs correspond to the two tangent segments on the hypotenuse.

Therefore:

\[
a=r+x
\]

\[

\[

\[

\[

\[
a+b=2r+c
\]

Rearrange:

\[
\boxed{r=\frac{a+b-c}{2}}
\]
