### 5. MATHEMATICAL DEFINITIONS AND NOTATION

Use consistent notation throughout the article.

Let triangle ABC be right-angled at C.

Define:

a = length of leg AC

b = length of leg BC

c = length of hypotenuse AB

h = altitude from C to AB

D = foot of the altitude on AB

p = length of hypotenuse segment AD

q = length of hypotenuse segment DB

Therefore:

c = p + q

The primary formulas are:

**Altitude from sides:**

h = ab/c

**Altitude from projections:**

h = √(pq)

**Pythagorean theorem:**

a² + b² = c²

**Altitude from two known legs:**

h = ab/√(a² + b²)

**Altitude from area and hypotenuse:**

h = 2A/c

where A represents the area.

**Hypotenuse projections:**

p = a²/c

q = b²/c

**Geometric mean relationship:**

h² = pq

These formulas must be explained accurately and used only under appropriate geometric conditions.
