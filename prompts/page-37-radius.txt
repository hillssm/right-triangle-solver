#### INRadius Formula

The main formula is:

r = (a + b − c)/2

An equivalent formula is:

r = (a × b)/(a + b + c)

For a right triangle, these two expressions are equivalent.

The semiperimeter is:

s = (a + b + c)/2

The inradius can also be calculated from:

r = Area/s

Since the area of a right triangle is:

Area = ab/2

Therefore:

r = ab/(a + b + c)

Explain why these formulas give the same result.

#### CIRCUMRADIUS FORMULA

The main formula is:

R = c/2

The equivalent diameter relationship is:

c = 2R

For a right triangle, the hypotenuse is the diameter of the circumcircle.

The circumcenter is located at the midpoint of the hypotenuse.

#### OTHER USEFUL RELATIONSHIPS

Pythagorean theorem:

a² + b² = c²

Area:

Area = ab/2

Perimeter:

P = a + b + c

Semiperimeter:

s = (a + b + c)/2

Altitude:

h = ab/c

Keep notation consistent.

Never confuse:

- r with R.
- Incenter with circumcenter.
- Incircle with circumcircle.
- Radius with diameter.
- Hypotenuse with perpendicular legs.
