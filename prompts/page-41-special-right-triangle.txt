## 5. MATHEMATICAL DEFINITIONS AND CONSISTENT NOTATION

Explain both special triangle types separately.

### A. 45-45-90 TRIANGLE

Consider a right triangle with:

- ∠A = 45°.
- ∠B = 45°.
- ∠C = 90°.

The two perpendicular legs are equal.

\[
a=b
\]

The side ratio is:

\[
\boxed{a:b:c=1:1:\sqrt{2}}
\]

**Hypotenuse from leg:**

\[
c=a\sqrt{2}
\]

**Leg from hypotenuse:**

\[
a=\frac{c}{\sqrt{2}}
\]

Equivalent:

\[
a=\frac{c\sqrt{2}}{2}
\]

**Pythagorean derivation:**

\[
a^2+a^2=c^2
\]

\[
2a^2=c^2
\]

\[
c=a\sqrt{2}
\]

### B. 30-60-90 TRIANGLE

Consider a right triangle with:

- ∠A = 30°.
- ∠B = 60°.
- ∠C = 90°.

Use:

- a = short leg opposite 30°.
- b = long leg opposite 60°.
- c = hypotenuse opposite 90°.

The side ratio is:

\[
\boxed{a:b:c=1:\sqrt{3}:2}
\]

**Long leg from short leg:**

\[
b=a\sqrt{3}
\]

**Hypotenuse from short leg:**

\[
c=2a
\]

**Short leg from hypotenuse:**

\[
a=\frac{c}{2}
\]

**Short leg from long leg:**

\[
a=\frac{b}{\sqrt{3}}
\]

**Long leg from hypotenuse:**

\[
b=\frac{c\sqrt{3}}{2}
\]

**Pythagorean derivation:**

\[
a^2+(a\sqrt{3})^2=(2a)^2
\]

\[
a^2+3a^2=4a^2
\]

### C. RELATED AREA FORMULAS
