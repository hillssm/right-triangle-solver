### A. DEFINITION OF SIMILAR TRIANGLES

Two triangles are similar when:

- Their corresponding angles are equal.
- Their corresponding side lengths are proportional.
- They have the same shape.
- They may have different sizes.

Explain that similar triangles do not need to have identical side lengths.

### B. AA SIMILARITY RULE

Both triangles are right triangles.

Therefore, each has one angle measuring:

\[
90^\circ
\]

If one additional acute angle in one triangle equals an acute angle in the other, the third angles must also match.

Therefore, the triangles are similar by the Angle-Angle (AA) criterion.

Explain why only one additional equal angle is needed when both triangles are already known to be right triangles.

Explain that a scale factor describes the direction from the original triangle to the similar triangle. Reversing the direction requires using the reciprocal.
